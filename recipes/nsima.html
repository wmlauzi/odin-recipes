Nsima

Nsima is a staple Malawian dish made from maize flour and commonly served with vegetables, beans, meat, or fish.

Ingredients

  • Water
  • Maize flour

Instructions

  1. Add 1 litre of water to a cooking pot on a stove.
  2. Add 2 cups of flour as the water is heating.
  3. Gradually stir until the mixture turns into a well-boiling porridge.
  4. Cover the pot and let the porridge boil for 15–20 minutes.
  5. Add more flour and mix until the desired consistency is reached.
  6. Cover the pot and let it simmer for a few minutes.
  7. After a few minutes, your nsima is ready to serve.
