Beef Stew

A hearty beef stew made with tender beef, tomatoes, onions, and simple seasonings.

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Water

Instructions

  1. Cut the beef into small pieces.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a cooking pot.
  3. Add the beef and brown it on all sides.
  4. Add chopped onion and garlic and cook until soft.
  5. Add chopped tomatoes and stir well.
  6. Add water and salt, then cover the pot.
  7. Cook until the beef is tender and the stew has thickened.
  8. Serve hot with nsima, rice, or vegetables.
