Chicken

A simple chicken dish that can be served with nsima, rice, vegetables, or potatoes.

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Water

Instructions

  1. Clean the chicken pieces and place them in a cooking pot.
  2. Add water and salt, then cook the chicken until tender.
  3. Heat cooking oil in a separate pan.
  4. Add chopped onion and garlic and cook until soft.
  5. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they form a sauce.
  6. Add the cooked chicken and mix well.
  7. Simmer for a few minutes and serve.
